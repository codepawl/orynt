# Asset provenance

- `fonts/Lora/` and `fonts/Outfit/` retain their upstream README and SIL Open
  Font License 1.1 text beside the font files.
- `landing/` and `pictures/` contain Orynt project artwork introduced and
  maintained in this repository.
- `brand/codepawl-orynt/` is the owner-provided source of truth for the
  CodePawl company brand and the Orynt product brand. It includes the editable
  tldraw source, production SVG/PNG exports, social banners, and visual QA
  sheets. Runtime copies and platform icons are derived from this kit by
  `bun brand:sync`.

Before changing the repository to public visibility, the owner must confirm
that the Orynt artwork is original, commissioned with redistribution rights, or
generated from inputs that permit Apache-2.0 redistribution. Unknown or
third-party artwork must be removed or receive explicit license documentation;
the absence of a source URL is not evidence of ownership. The repository owner
has approved the CodePawl × Orynt kit for inclusion in this public project.
